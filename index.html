Questions

Will callers know it's AI?

Sami greets callers naturally by name. If anyone asks whether they're talking to a person or AI, she always answers honestly that she's an AI assistant, and callers are told the call may be recorded. She never claims to be human. She's friendly and quick, and gets them helped.

Can it answer only missed or after-hours calls?

Yes. You choose: forward only the calls you don't pick up, only after-hours calls, or all calls. You can change it anytime.

What if a caller wants a human?

Sami takes their name, number and reason for calling and texts you right away so you can call back. If you'd like, urgent calls can be transferred to your cell.

Are calls recorded?

Yes, for quality and so we can send you accurate summaries. Every call starts with a short notice that the call may be recorded. Pennsylvania and some other states require everyone on the call to agree, so we always announce it.

Does it make sales calls or robocalls?

No. Callbassador only answers incoming calls and replies to texts from people who contacted you. It never cold-calls anyone.

Can I keep my number?

Yes. Keep your number and forward calls to us: all calls, after-hours calls, or only the ones you don't pick up.

What happens in an emergency?

You set the rules for what counts as an emergency. Sami flags it, texts you the details right after the call, and can transfer the caller to your cell.

How long does setup take?

Usually within 24–48 hours once we have your details. We do the setup, and you answer a few questions.

Does it work for cleaning companies?

Yes. Sami takes quote details (size, rooms, pets, how often), books walkthroughs on Pro and Crew plans, handles reschedules from recurring clients, and takes move-out and commercial requests. See how it works for cleaning businesses.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve home-service businesses across the United States and set everything up remotely. We may not be able to offer service in every state yet; if we can't serve yours, we'll tell you before your trial starts.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime. There are no contracts.

What does the trial cost?

$0.00 due today for your 14-day free trial, including free custom setup. Cancel anytime before Day 14 and you will not be charged.
