The Philadelphia remodeling market

General contracting in Philadelphia means rowhome renovations, kitchen and bath remodels, basement finishes, roof decks, and full gut rehabs in neighborhoods like Fishtown, Point Breeze and Brewerytown. Tight sites, party walls and limited parking make every estimate visit a planning exercise.

Towns and area codes we cover

Renovation projects here come from Manayunk, Center City, the Northeast, South Philly, Northern Liberties, Port Richmond, Fishtown, Chestnut Hill, Roxborough, from Mayfair to Somerton, University City, West Philly, Kensington, Germantown and Mt. Airy. Area codes: 215 and its overlays 267 and 445.

When the phones get busiest

Remodel inquiries pick up after the new year and again in late summer, when homeowners plan projects before the holidays. Spring and summer bring exterior work and roof decks. Water-damage repair calls come whenever a pipe bursts or a roof leaks, often in the middle of other jobs.

Licensing and permits, in brief

Contractors working in Philadelphia need a contractor license from the Department of Licenses and Inspections, and most renovation work needs a building permit. Statewide, home improvement contractors register with the Pennsylvania Attorney General. Businesses working in the city also need a Commercial Activity License. General background only; confirm current rules locally.
