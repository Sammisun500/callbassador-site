The Chester County, PA landscaping market

Chester County has some of the biggest residential lots in the Philadelphia suburbs, plus estate properties in Unionville and the Brandywine Valley, HOA communities in East Goshen and Uwchlan, and commercial campuses along Route 202. That means a mix of weekly mowing routes, big one-time cleanups and commercial contracts.

Towns and area codes we cover

Landscaping routes here typically cover Spring City, Oxford, West Chester, Berwyn, Kennett Square, Avondale, Phoenixville, Exton, West Grove, Downingtown, the townships around them, Malvern, Paoli and Coatesville. Area codes: 610 and its overlay 484.

When the phones get busiest

Calls bunch up hard. March and April bring spring cleanups, mulch and first-cut requests all at once, and fall brings leaf cleanup and aeration. Summer is steady maintenance plus patio and hardscape estimates. Winter flips to snow-removal questions, mostly from businesses and HOAs that want an answer fast before the first storm.

Licensing and permits, in brief

Landscapers doing residential work in Pennsylvania generally register as home improvement contractors with the Attorney General. Anyone applying pesticides or herbicides for hire needs a license from the Pennsylvania Department of Agriculture, and hardscape or drainage work may need a township permit. General background only; confirm current rules locally.
