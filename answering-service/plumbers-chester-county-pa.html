The Chester County, PA plumbing market

Chester County plumbing is a mix of a few very different jobs: farmhouses and stone twins on private wells and septic, 1970s and 1980s split-levels with aging water heaters, and newer subdivisions around Exton and Downingtown on public water. A caller in Elverson and a caller in Malvern can describe the same "no water" problem and mean completely different things.

Towns and area codes we cover

Plumbing shops we set up here typically run calls to Kennett Square, Coatesville, Avondale, Phoenixville, Spring City, Downingtown, Exton, Oxford, Paoli, West Chester, the townships around them, Berwyn, Malvern and West Grove. Area codes: 610 and its overlay 484.

When the phones get busiest

Winter is the crunch. When temperatures drop into the teens, pipes in crawl spaces, garages and exterior walls freeze, and the calls come when the thaw starts and the leaks show up. Spring brings sump pump and basement water calls after heavy rain, and well-pump failures tend to show up whenever the power flickers in a storm.

Licensing and permits, in brief

Pennsylvania has no statewide plumbing license, so licensing is handled locally, and many of the county's boroughs and townships enforce the Pennsylvania Uniform Construction Code for permits and inspections. Contractors doing residential work generally need to register with the Pennsylvania Attorney General under the Home Improvement Consumer Protection Act. General background only; confirm current rules locally.
