How Sami handles an emergency call

When a caller reports active leaks, storm damage and tarping requests, Sami treats it as urgent based on the rules you set. She asks where the leak is, how bad it is, whether there is storm damage, and the address, then texts you the details within moments of the call ending. If you choose, she can transfer the caller straight to your cell. Routine calls, like estimates and scheduling, go on your calendar or into a tidy summary so you can call back when you're off the ladder or out from under the sink.

Sami doesn't replace you or your office. Forward only the calls you miss, only after-hours calls, or all of them, and change it anytime. We set everything up for you, usually within 48 hours, using your service area, your trades and your emergency rules for Philadelphia.
