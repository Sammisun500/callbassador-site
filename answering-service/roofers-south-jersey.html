The South Jersey roofing market

Roofing in South Jersey ranges from asphalt-shingle roofs on suburban colonials in Burlington and Camden counties to shore homes in Atlantic County that face salt air and coastal wind. Many shore-house owners live out of state, so they call from a distance and want photos and a clear next step.

Towns and area codes we cover

Roofing crews here work rooftops in Deptford, Marlton, Collingswood, Medford, Vineland, Mount Laurel, Mullica Hill, Egg Harbor Township, Cherry Hill, Glassboro, Galloway, Moorestown, the shore towns from Brigantine to Ocean City, Haddonfield, Voorhees and Washington Township. Area codes: 856 in Camden, Gloucester and Cumberland counties, and 609 and its overlay 640 in Burlington and Atlantic counties and down the shore.

When the phones get busiest

Atlantic hurricane season runs from June through November, and coastal storms and nor'easters in fall and winter bring the biggest surges of leak and missing-shingle calls, often dozens on the same morning. Spring brings inspections and replacement quotes, and many owners want work finished before summer rentals start.

Licensing and permits, in brief

New Jersey requires roofing contractors doing residential work to register as home improvement contractors with the Division of Consumer Affairs, and roof replacements usually need a permit from the local construction office under the state Uniform Construction Code. General background only; confirm current rules locally.
