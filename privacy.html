What we collect

Information you send us (name, business, phone, email, website). For customers, we also collect call audio, transcripts and call details processed on your behalf to answer and summarize calls.

How we use it

To provide demos and the service, send call summaries, provide support, bill you, and improve quality. We do not sell personal information.

Call recording

Calls begin with a notice that they may be recorded. Recordings and transcripts are kept for 30 days (see below).

Text messages

We text only people who opted in or contacted us. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply. Mobile opt-in data is never shared with third parties for marketing.

Service providers

We use vendors for voice, telephony, messaging, payments and hosting, and they process data only to provide those services.

Website chat

If you chat with Sami, our AI assistant, on this site, the chat transcript is saved and processed by our chat provider so we can reply, follow up if you ask us to, and improve the service.

Vendors we use

We share personal information only with vendors that help us run the service, including: Retell AI (voice and chat AI, phone numbers; voice by ElevenLabs through Retell), Stripe (payments; we never see full card numbers), GitHub Pages (website hosting), FormSubmit (website forms), Zoho Mail (email), HubSpot (customer records) and Instantly (business email outreach). We'll update this list if it changes.

How long we keep recordings and transcripts

Call recordings, call transcripts and website chat transcripts are kept for 30 days and then deleted. You can ask us to delete them sooner by emailing hello@callbassador.com. If you are a Callbassador customer, you can export or save your own call summaries before the 30 days are up. Separately, the call summaries we text or email to you stay with you.

Do Not Track

Our site does not currently respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals, because there is no common standard for them. We do not allow third parties to track you across other websites through our site, and we do not sell or share personal information for targeted advertising.

Your choices, including California and Nevada residents

To review, correct or delete your information, or (for Nevada residents) to submit a request about the sale of covered information, email hello@callbassador.com. We will verify the request and respond. We do not sell personal information.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we update the "Last updated" date at the top and post the new version on this page. For material changes, we will also email current customers.
