How it works

Callbassador is done for you. Sami is an AI receptionist. You do not install an app or learn a dashboard. We configure the line, you approve it, and then you forward the calls you want answered.

On this website

  1. Your shop. Name, business, trade, mobile, and email. About two minutes in the signup form.
  2. Plan and alerts. Starter, Pro, or Crew, and whether Sami should take missed calls, after-hours calls, or every call. A free custom demo stays on the form if you want to hear the line before a card.
  3. Trial checkout. Stripe collects a card for the 14-day trial. $0.00 is due today. The same Payment Links the site already uses are unchanged.

After you sign up

We email a short setup questionnaire within 1 business day. A person on our team follows up. The questionnaire covers services, hours, service area, prices you are willing to quote, emergency rules, and who should get the text after each call. On Pro and Crew we also connect appointment booking.

We build the greeting and the answers. You can ask for changes by email. When you are ready, you place a test call. Sami does not answer live customer calls until you approve.

Forwarding, without giving up your number

You keep the number on the truck, the website, and the Google listing. Forward only the calls you do not pick up, only after-hours calls, or all calls. The exact taps depend on your carrier or VoIP host. We walk you through it. A longer guide is on the blog: how to forward calls to an AI receptionist.

What callers get

Sami answers, tells callers the call may be recorded, and answers from the facts you approved. Emergencies follow your rules and produce a text with the details. Sami does not promise a technician's arrival time. You decide when to call back.

Illustrations of a text, a call summary, and a booking are on the homepage. They use a fictional shop, Liberty Plumbing.
