About Callbassador

Callbassador is run by Callbassador LLC, a Pennsylvania limited liability company. The mailing address is 64 E Uwchlan Ave, Suite 241, Exton, PA 19341. Email hello@callbassador.com.

We set up Sami, an AI receptionist, for home-service businesses in all 50 states. Plumbing, HVAC, electrical, roofing, landscaping, cleaning, and the other trades on our site are all in scope. Setup is remote. You keep the business number customers already call.

Sami is an AI receptionist, not a person. If a caller asks, Sami says so. Every call starts with a notice that it may be recorded.

What we actually do

There is no dashboard to learn. We build and tune the receptionist for your services, hours, service area, the prices you are willing to quote, and your emergency rules. Appointment booking on Google Calendar is on the Pro and Crew plans. Jobber is planned, and it is not available yet.

After you sign up, we email a short setup questionnaire within 1 business day. A person on our team follows up. Sami does not answer live customer calls until you approve.

What we do not do

  • We do not place outbound sales calls or robocalls. Sami answers people who contacted the business.
  • We do not publish customer counts, star ratings, or logos. There is no customer list to show on this site.
  • We do not claim security certifications we do not hold. See Security for how calls and data are handled.

Plans, the trial, and any promotional rate are on the pricing page. The 14-day trial is $0.00 due today. A card is collected on Stripe, and we never store full card numbers.
