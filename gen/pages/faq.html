Questions

Will callers know it's AI?

Sami is an AI receptionist, not a person. She doesn't claim to be human, and if a caller asks, she tells them she's an AI.

Can it answer only missed or after-hours calls?

Yes. You choose: forward only the calls you do not pick up, only after-hours calls, or all calls. You can change it later.

What if a caller wants a human?

Sami takes their name, number, and reason for calling and texts you the details so you can call back. Urgent calls can be transferred to your cell if you set that rule. Sami does not promise how soon someone will arrive.

Are calls recorded?

Every call plays a short notice that the call may be recorded. Recording and transcription should begin only after the required notice and any required consent for that call. This page is not a warranty of all-party-consent compliance in every state. Recordings and transcripts are kept for 30 days. See the security page and the privacy policy.

Does it make sales calls or robocalls?

No. Callbassador only answers incoming calls and replies to texts from people who contacted you. It never cold-calls anyone.

Can I keep my number?

Yes. Keep your number and forward calls: all calls, after-hours calls, or only the ones you do not pick up. See how forwarding works.

What happens in an emergency?

You set the rules for what counts as an emergency. Sami flags it, texts you the details after the call, and can transfer the caller to your cell. The text does not include a promised arrival time. You decide the callback.

How does setup work?

We do the setup once we have your details, and you answer a short questionnaire. We email that questionnaire within 1 business day of signup. A person follows up. We tell you when Sami is ready to test. Sami does not answer live customer calls until you approve.

Does it work for cleaning companies?

Yes. Sami takes quote details such as size, rooms, pets, and how often, books walkthroughs on Pro and Crew, handles reschedule requests from recurring clients, and takes move-out and commercial requests. Cleaning companies.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve home-service businesses across the United States and set everything up remotely. We may not be able to offer service in every state yet. If we cannot serve yours, we tell you before the trial starts.

Card required; $0 due today. 14-day free trial. Plans start at $79/month, plus applicable tax, and auto-renew monthly after the trial unless you cancel before it ends. For cancellation help, email hello@callbassador.com. Plan prices are on the pricing page.

Is there a free demo without a card?

Yes. The signup form has a visible option, "Prefer a free custom demo first," beside the trial. You can also play the recorded example call on the homepage. The live browser demo, when it is turned on, is labeled and uses your microphone.
