Security and how calls are handled

This page describes what the service does today. It is not a certification, an audit report, or a legal warranty. The privacy policy is still marked draft pending attorney review. If the two pages ever disagree, ask us at hello@callbassador.com and we will correct the public page.

Sami is an AI receptionist

Sami answers inbound calls for the business. Sami is an AI receptionist, not a person. She doesn't claim to be human, and if a caller asks, she tells them she's an AI. Callbassador does not place outbound sales calls or robocalls.

Every call plays a short notice that the call may be recorded. Recording and transcription should begin only after the required notice and any required consent for that call. This page is not a warranty of all-party-consent compliance in every state.

What a call collects

For a customer, we process call audio, a transcript, and the details needed to summarize the call: caller name, number, address, and the reason for the call, as the caller provides them. You set the emergency rules. The text or email summary is sent to the people you name. Sami does not promise a technician's arrival time on the call.

You approve the setup before Sami answers live customer calls.

How long recordings are kept

Call recordings, call transcripts, and website chat transcripts are kept for 30 days and then deleted. You can ask us to delete them sooner by emailing hello@callbassador.com. A customer can save their own summaries before the 30 days are up. The summaries we text or email to the business stay with that business. That retention rule is also in the privacy policy, last updated October 19, 2026.

Payments

Trial and subscription checkout runs on Stripe. The card form is Stripe's. We do not see or store full card numbers. The site is served over HTTPS. We do not claim PCI DSS certification for Callbassador LLC.

Website forms and messages

The signup form posts to FormSubmit, which emails the details to us. The privacy policy also names these vendors as processing data to run the service: enterprise voice, telephony, and chat providers; Stripe; GitHub Pages for the website; FormSubmit; Zoho Mail; HubSpot for customer records; and Instantly for business email outreach. We do not sell personal information.

Texts are sent to people who opted in or who contacted the business. Reply STOP to opt out. Mobile opt-in data is not shared with third parties for their marketing.

This website's measurement tools

The marketing site loads the Meta Pixel, which can set cookies and measure page views and signup events. A separate script, assets/cb-analytics.js, counts page events without its own cookie, including visits, form starts, and clicks on the voice demo. The privacy policy says the site does not respond to browser Do Not Track signals, because there is no common standard for them.
